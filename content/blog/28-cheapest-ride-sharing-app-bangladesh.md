Everyone in Dhaka has a favourite app, and everyone has a story about paying too much. So which ride sharing app is actually the cheapest? The honest answer is that no app wins every trip. The cheapest choice depends on how each app sets its price, what time you travel, and which vehicle you pick. This guide explains all three so you can pay less on every ride.

## Why there is no single cheapest app

Ride apps in Bangladesh use three very different pricing models:

1. **Rate card with surge.** A base fare plus a per km and per minute rate, which can rise when demand is high. Uber and Pathao work this way.
2. **Bidding.** You offer a price and drivers accept or counter. inDrive works this way.
3. **Upfront fixed fare.** The app shows the exact fare before you book and there is no bargaining. Arohon works this way.

Each model can be the cheapest on a given trip. A rate card app can be cheap at 2 pm on a Friday and expensive at 6 pm on a Sunday. A bidding app can be cheap if drivers are hungry for trips, and slow if they are not.

There is also a legal ceiling. Under the Ride Sharing Service Policy 2017, ride share fares may not be higher than taxi cab fares. That does not make any app cheap, but it limits how far prices can go.

## The cheapest mode for each type of trip

The vehicle you pick changes the price far more than the app. A Policy Research Institute estimate put a typical bike trip under ৳150, a CNG trip around ৳250 and a car trip around ৳300.

| Trip | Cheapest sensible choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Solo, short city trip under 8 km | Bike or EV bike | Lowest fare and fastest in traffic |
| Solo, with a bag or in rain | CNG | Cheaper than a car, keeps you dry |
| Two or three people | CNG or car | Split the fare and it beats two bikes |
| Family of four to six | Car plus or micro | One vehicle instead of two |
| Group trip or Eid travel | Hiace | Lowest cost per seat for big groups |
| Out of town for a day | Rental | Compare offers from several drivers |

The rule is simple: the cheapest ride is the smallest vehicle that fits your people and luggage safely.

## How bidding compares with fixed fares

Bidding can feel cheaper because you name the number. inDrive's strength is that control. If you know a route well and travel at a quiet hour, you can often get a fair deal, and some drivers like that they can see the offer before accepting.

The trade off is time and certainty. A low offer can sit unaccepted while you wait on the roadside. At rush hour you may have to raise your offer several times. And you need to know what a fair price is, which is hard on an unfamiliar route.

A fixed upfront fare removes that guessing. You see the price, you book, and the driver cannot ask for more at pickup.

## How surge changes the answer

Rate card apps raise prices when many people request rides and few drivers are online. Uber's own Dhaka blog explains that dynamic pricing may raise fares for a while, and that passengers see a notice in the app when this happens. Peak hours, rain and Eid are the usual triggers. On a surge evening, the app that was cheapest in the morning can be the most expensive.

## Hidden costs that make a cheap fare expensive

1. **Bargaining at pickup.** A driver who asks for "a little extra" turns a cheap fare into an average one.
2. **Cancellations.** Waiting 15 minutes and rebooking costs you time and often a higher price.
3. **Wrong vehicle.** Two bikes for two people can cost more than one CNG.
4. **Ignoring the time fare.** On per minute pricing, a jam adds money even when you are not moving.

## Simple ways to pay less on any app

1. **Open two or three apps and compare** before you book.
2. **Travel 30 minutes earlier or later** than the office rush.
3. **Walk to a main road.** Pickups on busy roads are faster and avoid long detours.
4. **Share a car or CNG** with colleagues going the same way.
5. **Use referral rewards.** On Arohon, passengers earn ৳10 cash for every friend they refer.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which is the cheapest ride sharing app in Bangladesh?

No single app is cheapest on every trip. Rate card apps can be cheap at quiet hours and expensive during surge, while bidding apps depend on drivers accepting your offer. Compare the live price in two or three apps before you book.

### Is inDrive cheaper than Pathao and Uber?

Sometimes. Because you propose the fare, inDrive can be cheaper at quiet times on routes you know. At rush hour you may need to raise your offer, which can wipe out the saving.

### What is the cheapest way to travel in Dhaka by app?

For one person, a bike or EV bike is usually the cheapest. For two or three people, a shared CNG or car often costs less per person than separate bikes.

### Why is my ride fare higher in the evening?

Evening rush hour brings more requests than available drivers, so apps with dynamic or peak pricing raise fares. Travelling earlier or later usually brings the price down.

### Does Arohon have surge pricing?

The Arohon app shows the exact fare before you book, so you know the price before you confirm, and there is no bargaining with the driver.
