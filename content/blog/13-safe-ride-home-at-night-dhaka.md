It is 11 PM, the office in Mohakhali has emptied out, and the street outside is quiet. The few CNGs waiting at the corner quote double the usual price, the buses have thinned out, and your phone battery is at 15%. Anyone who works late, studies late or lands at Shahjalal Airport after midnight knows this feeling. Getting home at night in Dhaka is not only about cost. It is about knowing who you are riding with and making sure someone knows where you are.

This guide covers the simple habits that make a late night trip safer, and how to plan a ride so you are not making decisions on a dark roadside.

## Why late night trips feel riskier

Night travel in Dhaka has a few common problems:

- **Fewer options.** Buses and leguna routes thin out, and the vehicles still on the road know it.
- **Street bargaining in the dark.** Standing on a footpath arguing over price draws attention and keeps you waiting longer than you want.
- **Unknown drivers.** A CNG or car picked off the street has no record of who the driver is or which route you took.
- **No one knows your route.** If something goes wrong, family may not know where you were or which vehicle you were in.

None of these mean you should avoid going out. They mean a bit of planning goes a long way.

## Plan the trip before you leave

The safest night ride is the one you organise while you are still indoors.

1. **Book from inside.** Request the ride while you are still in the office, restaurant or hall, and walk out only when the driver is close.
2. **Charge your phone.** Keep at least enough battery for the trip, the call to the driver and a call home. A small power bank helps.
3. **Pick a clear pickup point.** Choose a lit, busy spot such as a building gate, a shop front or a known landmark rather than a dark side lane.
4. **Know the rough fare.** Seeing the fare before you book means no argument on the street.
5. **Tell someone your plan.** A quick message saying "leaving Gulshan now, home in 40 minutes" is a habit worth building.

## Check the driver before you get in

This is the single most important step at night. Before you open the door or sit on the bike:

| Check | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Name | Matches the name shown in the app |
| Photo | The person driving is the person in the photo |
| Number plate | Every digit matches the plate in the app |
| Vehicle type | A CNG when you booked a CNG, a car when you booked a car |

If anything does not match, do not board. Cancel and book again. A polite driver will understand, and a real driver will not pressure you to get in.

Also, never accept a ride from someone who approaches you saying "I am your driver" before you have checked the plate yourself.

## Share your trip with family

Sharing your live trip lets a parent, partner or friend follow the ride on their phone until you reach home. For someone heading from Uttara to Mirpur at midnight, or a student coming back from a late class, this small step gives real peace of mind on both ends.

Make it routine rather than something you do only when you feel nervous. When it is a habit, you never forget it on the night it matters.

## Choosing the right vehicle at night

Different vehicles suit different night trips:

- **Car.** Doors close, windows can stay up, and it is the most private option for longer trips such as Shahjalal Airport to Dhanmondi.
- **CNG.** A good middle choice for short to medium trips. Choose a booked CNG with a known driver over one picked off the street.
- **Bike.** Fast in light night traffic, but you are exposed. Always wear the helmet and choose this for shorter trips on main roads.
- **Micro.** Useful when a group is heading the same way, for example friends leaving a wedding together.

## During the ride

A few small habits keep you in control:

- Sit in the back seat of a car, behind the passenger side.
- Keep your phone in hand and the map open so you can follow the route.
- Keep your bag close and avoid showing cash or valuables.
- If the route changes for no clear reason, ask the driver politely why. Main roads are usually better at night even if slightly longer.
- Trust your instinct. If you feel uncomfortable, ask to stop at a busy, lit place such as a petrol pump or a market.

## Arriving home

Ask the driver to stop right at your gate or building entrance rather than at the corner. Wait until you are inside before you put your phone away, and send a quick "reached" message to whoever was following your trip.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the safest way to travel at night in Dhaka?

Book a ride from indoors, check the driver's name, photo and plate before boarding, share your live trip with family, and choose a car for longer trips. Avoid bargaining with vehicles on the street after dark.

### Is a bike ride safe late at night?

A bike can be quick when roads are empty, but you are more exposed than in a car. For short trips on main roads with a helmet it can work well. For long trips or very late hours, many passengers prefer a car or CNG.

### What should I do if the driver does not match the app?

Do not get in. Cancel the ride and book again. The name, photo and number plate shown in the app should always match the person and vehicle in front of you.
