Your bike already takes you to class or the office every day. It also sits parked most of the evening while rent, fuel and family costs keep rising. For many students and salaried workers in Dhaka, a few hours of riding in the morning or evening has become a simple way to add to the month's budget, without quitting anything. Here is how to do it well.

## Why part time riding works as a side income

1. **You already own the tool.** No shop, no stock, no big investment.
2. **You choose the hours.** Ride before class, after office or on Friday. Stop when you want.
3. **You get paid daily.** Rides are cash only, so you take your money home after each trip.
4. **Your commute can pay.** A passenger going the same way as you covers part of your fuel.

## Fit riding around your day

The best side income plan is one that does not hurt your studies or main job.

**For students**

1. Ride between classes or for two hours in the evening.
2. Pick areas near your campus, where you know the roads well.
3. Keep exam weeks free. Your degree comes first.

**For office workers**

1. Turn the app on for your commute to and from work.
2. Do a few trips after office, when demand is high around Gulshan, Banani, Mohakhali and Motijheel.
3. Use Friday mornings if you want a few extra hours.

Set a weekly target, for example a fixed number of hours, and stick to it. Riding while tired is not worth any amount of money.

## Smart habits that raise your earnings

1. **Know the busy spots.** Office areas, universities, hospitals, Shahjalal Airport, and terminals like Sayedabad, Gabtoli and Kamalapur bring steady trips.
2. **Keep your bike in good shape.** Check brakes, tyres and lights every week. A breakdown costs both money and time.
3. **Carry a clean spare helmet.** Passengers notice, and it is the safe thing to do.
4. **Be polite and on time.** Good ratings lead to more trips.
5. **Track your income and fuel.** A simple note on your phone shows what you really earn each week.
6. **Have change ready.** Since rides are cash, keep small notes for passengers.

## Plan your costs before you start

Side income is only real after costs. Before you begin, write down what a typical riding day costs you: fuel, a share of servicing, mobile data and any parking. Compare that with what you keep after the 2% commission, the ৳3 safety charge and the booking fee. If the numbers work for two hours a day, you have a plan you can repeat every week. If they do not, change your hours or your area before you change anything else.

## Safety first

Follow traffic rules, wear your helmet, and make sure your passenger wears one too. Avoid riding in very heavy rain. Arohon passengers can see your name, photo and number plate before boarding, and can share their trip with family, which builds trust for both sides.
