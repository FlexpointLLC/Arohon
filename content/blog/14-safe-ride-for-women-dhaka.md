Every woman who travels in Dhaka has her own list of worries. The crowded bus where someone stands too close. The CNG driver who takes an unfamiliar lane. The bike ride where you are not sure the person matches the photo. Whether you are heading to class in Dhanmondi, an office in Gulshan or coming home to Mirpur after work, you deserve to feel in control of the trip.

This checklist is meant to be practical. Save it, share it with a sister or friend, and turn the steps into habits.

## Before you book

A safe trip starts before the vehicle arrives.

1. **Choose the vehicle that suits the trip.** A car gives the most privacy. A CNG works well for short trips. A bike is quick but you sit very close to the driver, so choose it only when you are comfortable.
2. **Book from a safe place.** Request the ride while you are inside the office, campus or shop, not while standing alone on the road.
3. **Set a clear pickup point.** A gate, a shop front or a well known landmark is better than a quiet corner.
4. **Check the fare upfront.** Knowing the price before you book removes the need to negotiate with a stranger.
5. **Charge your phone.** Keep enough battery for the whole ride and a call home.

## When the driver arrives

This is the step many people skip when they are in a hurry. Do not skip it.

| Check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Driver name | Confirms you have the right booking |
| Driver photo | Confirms the person is the registered driver |
| Number plate | Confirms the vehicle is the one assigned to you |
| Vehicle type and colour | A quick visual match before reading the plate |

If anything does not match, do not get in. Cancel and book again. You do not need to explain or apologise.

A useful habit: instead of saying your own name, ask the driver "Who are you here to pick up?" A real driver will know.

## Share your trip, every time

Send your live trip to a family member or a trusted friend before the ride starts. They can follow your route on their phone until you reach. This is especially helpful for:

- Students travelling between campus and home
- Women working late shifts in Mohakhali, Banani or Motijheel
- Trips to and from Shahjalal Airport at odd hours
- Visits to unfamiliar parts of the city

Doing it on every ride, not only at night, means you never forget it when it matters most.

## During the ride

Small actions keep you aware and in control:

- **Sit in the back** of a car, on the side opposite the driver.
- **Keep the map open** on your phone and follow the route.
- **Keep calls short and general.** Avoid saying your full address or that you live alone within earshot.
- **Keep valuables out of sight.**
- **Speak up early.** If the driver takes an unexpected turn, ask politely. A clear question often settles things.
- **Know where to stop.** If you feel uneasy, ask to be dropped at a busy, lit place such as a market, petrol pump or hospital gate.

## On a bike ride

Bike rides are popular for beating traffic, and many women use them daily. A few extra points help:

- Always wear the helmet provided.
- Sit comfortably and hold the rear grip rather than the driver.
- Keep your bag on your lap or in front, not hanging loose.
- Choose main roads for longer trips.

## At drop off

- Ask to be dropped at your gate or building entrance, not down the lane.
- Pay, take your bag and step away before checking your phone.
- Send a "reached" message to whoever was following the trip.

## A quick checklist to save

## Frequently asked questions

### Which vehicle is safest for women in Dhaka?

For privacy, a car is usually the most comfortable choice because you sit in the back with doors closed. A CNG is a good option for short trips. Whatever you choose, checking the driver and sharing your trip matter most.

### What should I check before getting into a ride?

Check that the driver's name, photo and number plate match what the app shows. If anything is different, do not board, and book again.

### Is it safe to take a bike ride as a woman?

Many women take bike rides every day in Dhaka. Wear the helmet, hold the rear grip, keep your bag in front, and choose main roads for longer trips. If you are not comfortable, pick a CNG or car instead.
