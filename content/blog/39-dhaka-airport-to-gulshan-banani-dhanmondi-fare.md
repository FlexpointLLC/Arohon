You land at Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport, clear immigration, and walk out into a crowd of drivers calling out prices. Knowing roughly what a fair fare looks like before you step outside is the easiest way to avoid paying double. This guide covers the most common routes from the airport, using published figures and clear ranges where exact numbers are not published.

## The quick answer

Travel guides put a ride app car from the airport to Gulshan at roughly ৳600 to ৳1,200, depending on traffic, time of day and car type. A CNG for the same trip is usually quoted at ৳400 to ৳600 after bargaining. Those are the most widely cited figures, and the table below scales them by distance for other areas.

## Why the same trip can cost double

1. **Traffic.** Airport Road and the Banani to Mohakhali stretch can turn a 25 minute trip into more than an hour. Time based pricing adds up.
2. **Time of arrival.** Late night arrivals have fewer drivers around, while evening rush hour has heavy demand.
3. **Car type.** A larger car or premium option costs more than a standard sedan.
4. **Surge on some apps.** Some apps raise prices when demand is high. Read our guide on surge pricing for more.
5. **Touts at the exit.** Unbooked offers inside the terminal are often the most expensive.

## Airport to Uttara

Uttara is the closest major area to the airport, so this is the cheapest of the common trips. For a solo traveller with light luggage, a bike ride is often the lowest cost option. With suitcases, book a car.

## Airport to Gulshan and Banani

These are the most searched routes, for hotels, embassies and offices. Expect the travel guide range of ৳600 to ৳1,200 by ride app car. Banani is slightly closer than Gulshan 2 for most addresses. If you arrive in the evening, the Kakoli and Banani junctions can be slow, so leave buffer time if you have a meeting.

## Airport to Dhanmondi

Dhanmondi is on the other side of the city, and the trip crosses some of Dhaka's busiest corridors. It is one of the longer airport rides, and the fare reflects that. The Dhaka Elevated Expressway can save a lot of time when your driver uses it, though tolls apply.

## Shahjalal Airport to Motijheel

Motijheel is the main business district in the south. A car is the easy option with luggage. Without luggage, the metro rail is worth considering: Dhaka metro fares are set at ৳5 per km, with a minimum of ৳20 and a maximum of ৳100 for the full Uttara North to Motijheel run (Prothom Alo, Daily Star). You would still need a short ride from the airport to an Uttara station.

| Option to Motijheel | Cost | Best for |

| Ride app car | Higher, varies with traffic | Travellers with bags |
| CNG | Agreed by bargaining | Short budgets, light bags |
