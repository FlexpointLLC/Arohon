You check the app at 2 pm and a trip to Mirpur looks reasonable. At 6 pm the same trip costs far more. Nothing about the distance changed. What changed is demand. This guide explains why ride fares jump at rush hour in Dhaka, what the rules say, and how to avoid paying surge.

## What surge pricing is

Surge pricing, also called dynamic or peak pricing, is when a ride app raises its fares because many people want rides and few drivers are free nearby. The higher price is meant to do two things: bring more drivers onto the road, and reduce requests from people who can wait.

Uber's own Dhaka blog explains that dynamic pricing may temporarily raise fares, and that passengers in Dhaka are notified in the app when fares are higher than normal. Pathao uses peak pricing in a similar way.

## Why fares jump at rush hour in Dhaka

1. **Everyone leaves at once.** Offices in Gulshan, Banani, Motijheel and Karwan Bazar close at similar times, so thousands of requests arrive together.
2. **Traffic slows every driver.** A driver stuck in a jam cannot pick up the next passenger, so supply shrinks just when demand grows.
3. **Time based fares add up.** On per minute pricing, a slow trip costs more even without surge.
4. **Drivers avoid some routes.** Trips deep into gridlock may get fewer takers, which pushes prices higher.

## Other surge triggers

| Trigger | Why prices rise |
|---|---|
| Rain and waterlogging | Fewer drivers go out, more people want covered rides |
| Eid and long holidays | Many drivers go home, demand for terminals and airport spikes |
| Big events | Cricket matches, concerts and fairs end at the same time |
| Late night | Fewer drivers online |
| Fuel price changes | Apps may raise base rates, as Pathao did in 2022 (The Daily Star, 2022) |

In 2019, The Daily Star reported passenger complaints about Uber surge fares during the Eid holidays, even on traffic free days.

## What the rules say

The Ride Sharing Service Policy 2017 says ride share fares may not be higher than taxi cab fares. The Taxicab Service Guidelines 2010 set cab fares at ৳85 for the first 2 km and ৳34 for each km after that. That works as a ceiling, but it still leaves room for big swings on everyday trips.

## How to avoid surge pricing

1. **Shift your time by 30 minutes.** Leaving at 4:30 pm or 7:30 pm instead of 6 pm often avoids the worst of it.
2. **Wait 10 to 15 minutes.** Surge is often short. Refresh the app before you book.
3. **Walk away from the hotspot.** Prices outside a busy mall, office tower or stadium can be higher than one road away.
4. **Pick a smaller vehicle.** A bike or CNG has more supply than cars at rush hour.
5. **Compare apps.** One app may be surging while another is not.
6. **Share a ride.** Three colleagues in one car split any surge.
7. **Plan around rain.** Read our guide to [riding in rain and waterlogging](https://blogs.arohon.co/ride-in-rain-dhaka-waterlogging).
8. **Book early for airport and Eid trips.** Do not leave it to the last minute.

## Surge vs bidding vs fixed fares

| Model | Who sets the price | What happens at rush hour |
|---|---|---|
| Surge or peak pricing (Uber, Pathao) | The app's rate card plus a multiplier | Fare rises automatically |
| Bidding (inDrive) | You offer, drivers counter | You may need to raise your offer |
| Upfront fixed fare (Arohon) | The app shows the exact fare before you book | You see the price before you confirm |

Each model has a logic. Surge brings more drivers out. Bidding lets you negotiate. A fixed upfront fare gives you certainty.

## Is surge fair?

There are two sides. For drivers, rush hour trips take longer and burn more fuel, so higher pay at those times is reasonable. For passengers, a sudden jump with no warning feels unfair, especially in rain or on Eid. The fairest systems are the ones that tell you the price clearly before you book.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is Uber fare so high at rush hour?

Uber uses dynamic pricing. When many passengers request rides and few drivers are free, fares rise for a while. Uber notifies passengers in Dhaka when fares are higher.

### What time is peak hour for ride apps in Dhaka?

Usually around 8 am to 10 am and 5 pm to 8 pm on working days, plus during rain, holidays and big events.

### How can I avoid surge pricing in Dhaka?

Travel 30 minutes earlier or later, wait 10 to 15 minutes for the surge to drop, walk away from busy hotspots, choose a bike or CNG, and compare apps.

### Is surge pricing legal in Bangladesh?

The 2017 ride sharing policy says ride share fares may not exceed taxi cab fares. Within that limit, apps set their own prices.

### Does Pathao have surge pricing?

Yes, Pathao uses peak pricing when demand is high. The app shows the fare before you confirm.
