The sky opens at 5 PM, just as you leave the office. Within half an hour the road outside is ankle deep, the rickshaw puller asks for three times the usual fare, and every CNG either refuses or names a price you cannot believe. If you live in Dhaka, you know this routine every monsoon. Media reports in 2025 described rickshaw and CNG fares doubling or tripling during heavy rain as waterlogging spread across the city.

You cannot stop the rain, but you can avoid being stuck in it. Here is how to plan a rain day trip.

## Why getting a ride is so hard in the rain

A few things happen at once when Dhaka floods:

- **Demand jumps.** Everyone who would have walked or taken a bus now wants a covered vehicle.
- **Supply drops.** Some drivers stop working, and others avoid areas where water is deep.
- **Traffic slows.** Waterlogged roads and stalled vehicles turn short trips into long ones.
- **Street prices rise.** With many people waiting, street fares are set by whoever is most desperate.

## Areas that often flood

Reports over recent years have regularly named low lying parts of the city where water collects after heavy rain. Many passengers watch for trouble around:

- Mirpur, especially side roads off the main avenue
- Malibagh, Shantinagar and Moghbazar
- Old Dhaka lanes
- Parts of Mohakhali and Tejgaon
- Roads near Kamalapur and Motijheel

Conditions change every season, so check local news or ask colleagues who travel the same route.

## Plan before the rain hits

1. **Watch the forecast.** If heavy rain is expected in the afternoon, consider leaving early or staying a little later until the worst passes.
2. **Book before you step out.** Request the ride while you are still indoors and dry.
3. **Pick a pickup point on a main road.** Drivers can reach a main road far more easily than a flooded lane.
4. **Keep your phone dry.** A zip bag or small pouch protects it so you can still follow the driver and the route.
5. **Allow extra time.** A trip that takes 30 minutes on a dry day may take an hour or more.

## Which vehicle to choose in the rain

| Vehicle | Rain day pros | Rain day cons |
|---|---|---|
| Car | Fully covered, keeps you and your bags dry | Can get stuck in very deep water, slower in jams |
| CNG | Covered on most sides, handles moderate water | Engine can stall in deep water, sides let in some rain |
| Bike | Weaves through traffic | You will get wet, slippery roads, not ideal in heavy rain |
| Micro or Hiace | Sits higher, good for groups and luggage | Larger vehicle, slower in narrow lanes |

For most rain day trips, a car or CNG is the practical choice. Save the bike for a light drizzle on main roads.

## Safety tips on flooded roads

- **Do not walk through deep water** if you can avoid it. Open drains and manholes are hidden under the surface.
- **Do not push a driver** to enter water that looks too deep. A stalled vehicle helps no one.
- **Keep away from loose wires** and electric poles in standing water.
- **On a bike,** hold on firmly, keep your feet up in water, and ask the driver to go slowly.
- **Carry a small umbrella and a spare plastic bag** for shoes or documents.

## Avoid paying double or triple

The biggest rain day frustration is the price. On the street, there is no fixed number, and you are bargaining while getting soaked. A few ways to protect yourself:

- **Know the fare before you board.** Booking with an upfront fare means the price is agreed before the driver arrives.
- **Do not agree to a price in a hurry.** If you must take a street vehicle, agree the full amount before you sit down, including any extra for waiting.
- **Share the ride.** If colleagues are heading the same way, a micro or car shared between a few people makes more sense than separate rides.

## Sending things instead of going yourself

Sometimes the best trip in the rain is the one you do not take. If you need a parcel sent across the city, or food and medicine brought home, a delivery can save you a soaking.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why do fares go up so much when it rains in Dhaka?

More people want covered vehicles while fewer drivers are on the road and traffic slows down. On the street, that pushes prices up quickly. Reports in 2025 described rickshaw and CNG fares doubling or tripling in heavy rain.

### What is the best vehicle to take in heavy rain?

A car keeps you fully dry and is the most comfortable choice. A CNG is a good option for moderate water. Bikes are best avoided in heavy rain.

### Will the fare change on Arohon when it rains?

The fare is shown before you book, so you know the price before the driver arrives and there is no bargaining on the street.

### Is it safe to ride through waterlogged roads?

Moderate water is common, but deep water can stall vehicles and hide open drains. Do not push a driver into water that looks too deep, and avoid walking through flooded lanes where possible.

### Can I get medicine delivered when roads are flooded?

Yes, you can order medicine through Arohon and pay by cash, bKash, Nagad or card. Delivery times can be longer when roads are badly waterlogged.
