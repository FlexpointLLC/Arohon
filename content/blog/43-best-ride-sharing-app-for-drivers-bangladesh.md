If you drive a bike, CNG or car in Dhaka, the app you choose decides how much of every fare you take home. Two apps can show the same fare to a passenger and still leave you with very different amounts at the end of the day. This guide compares what the main apps in Bangladesh are known to charge drivers, using only published figures, and shows the difference in taka.

## What "best app for drivers" really means

A good app for drivers does four things well:

1. **Takes a small, predictable cut.** You should know before the trip what the app will keep.
2. **Pays you quickly.** Waiting days for a payout hurts when fuel is bought daily.
3. **Sends steady trips.** Low commission means little if the phone never rings.
4. **Treats the passenger fairly.** Upfront fares with no bargaining mean fewer arguments at pickup.

Commission is the easiest of these to compare, so we start there.

## Getting paid

On Arohon, rides are cash only. The passenger pays you directly at the end of the trip, so there is no payout to wait for and no wallet balance to withdraw. For drivers who buy fuel every morning, cash in hand at the end of each trip keeps things simple.

## Fewer arguments, better ratings

Passengers on Arohon see the fare before they book. There is no bargaining at pickup and no surprise at drop off. That matters to drivers too, because most bad ratings start with a disagreement about money. When both sides already agreed on the fare, the trip is just a trip.

Passengers also see your name, photo and plate before pickup and can share their live trip with family. That builds trust, especially for late evening rides.

## Things to check before you switch

1. **Read the fee screen.** Any app should show you what it keeps on each trip. If it does not, ask.
2. **Watch for hidden costs.** Subscriptions, deposits or weekly fees add up, even when commission looks low.
3. **Check demand in your area.** Try an app for a week in the places you usually ride.
4. **Keep your papers ready.** Licence, registration, national ID and a smartphone are the usual basics.

Many drivers run more than one app. Comparing your weekly take home on each is the fastest way to find your real answer.
