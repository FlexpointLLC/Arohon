A car ride is the most comfortable way to move around Dhaka. You get air conditioning, a closed cabin and room for bags or family. It is also the most expensive ride type, so a small difference in fare adds up fast if you take cars often. Which app actually gives you the cheapest car ride? The honest answer is that it changes trip by trip, but you can learn how each app prices a car and use that to pay less.

## How much does a car ride cost in Dhaka?

As a rough guide, a Policy Research Institute estimate put an average car ride in Dhaka at around ৳300, compared with about ৳250 for a CNG and under ৳150 for a bike. Long trips, rush hour and rain push that number higher.

## How each app prices a car ride

### Uber

Uber shows an upfront fare based on distance, time and demand. Its own notes say example prices are averages and real fares change with traffic, geography and promotions. Uber is widely used in Dhaka for car rides and also offers intercity trips.

### Pathao Car

Pathao Car also uses a fixed fare shown before booking. Pathao is better known for bikes, but car rides are available in the same app. We could not find a published 2026 per km car rate.

### inDrive

On inDrive, you name your price and drivers respond. This can be the cheapest option at quiet times if you offer a fair but low price. At rush hour, drivers often ignore low offers, so you may wait or have to raise the fare. Our [inDrive guide](https://blogs.arohon.co/indrive-bangladesh-guide) explains how to bargain sensibly.

### Arohon

Arohon offers Car and Car Plus, plus Micro and Hiace for groups. The app shows the exact fare before you book, so there is no bargaining at pickup, and rides are paid in cash. You see the driver's name, photo and plate before pickup and can share your trip live.

On the driver side, Arohon charges a flat 2% commission, a ৳3 safety charge and a booking fee of ৳5 to ৳20 depending on fare. On a ৳500 car trip, that is ৳10 commission, ৳3 safety charge and ৳20 booking fee, so the driver keeps ৳467. Drivers who keep more of each fare have less reason to cancel or ask passengers for extra.

## Five ways to get the cheapest car ride

1. **Compare two apps.** Open two apps and check the upfront price for the same trip before booking.
2. **Avoid peak hours.** Fares and waits rise between 8 and 10 in the morning and 5 and 8 in the evening.
3. **Walk to a main road.** A pickup on a main road saves the driver time and sometimes saves you money.
4. **Share with friends.** A car split between three or four people can cost less per person than three bikes.
5. **Use rental for long days.** If you need a car for several stops, a rental can beat many single trips. On Arohon rental, drivers send offers and you choose by price, rating and car. See [Arohon rental](https://arohon.co/services/rental).

## Car or Car Plus?

A standard car is fine for most city trips. Car Plus is for when you want a bigger or more comfortable car, for example for an airport run with luggage or for elderly parents. Pay for the upgrade only when it matters.

### Which app has the cheapest car ride in Dhaka?

No app is always cheapest. inDrive can be lower off peak if you bargain, and fixed fare apps like Uber, Pathao and Arohon are more predictable. Compare the upfront fare in two apps for each trip.

Rest of World reported UberX at ৳50 base plus ৳21 per km in 2022. Uber does not publish a current Dhaka rate card, and demand pricing can change the fare.

### What is the Pathao car fare in Dhaka?

Pathao has not published a current per km car rate that we could verify. The app shows the fare before you book.

### How much does an AC car ride cost in Dhaka?

A Policy Research Institute estimate put an average car trip at around ৳300. Your fare depends on distance, traffic and time of day.

Rides on Arohon are cash only. Card, bKash and Nagad are accepted for food and medicine orders.
