Sometimes a single ride is not enough. You need a car and driver for the whole day, a reliable office commute for the month, or a decorated car for a wedding. Dhaka has hundreds of rent a car shops, but comparing them means calling around and hoping the price does not change on the day.

| **Daily** | Day trips, shopping, family visits |
| **Weekly or monthly** | Office commute, school runs, executives |
| **Premium with chauffeur** | Important guests, business days |

## What is usually included

Ask every provider exactly what the price covers:

- **The driver's time**, and what happens after normal hours
- **Fuel**, or whether you pay separately
- **Tolls and parking**, usually paid by the customer
- **Driver's meals and stay** on overnight trips

## Monthly car rental for offices

For daily office commutes, a monthly hire gives you the **same driver every day**. Attendance is recorded in the app, off days are agreed upfront, and you always know who is coming. For teams, **Arohon for Business** adds team transport and deliveries in one account.

## Wedding cars

Weddings run on time, so your car should too. Book early, especially in the wedding season from November to February, confirm the decoration plan and the number of hours, and ask for a convoy if the family needs several cars.

## Tips to get a fair price

- **Compare offers**, not just the first quote.
- **Be specific** about hours and route so there are no surprises.
- **Check the driver's rating** and the car model.
- **Agree tolls and parking** before the day.
