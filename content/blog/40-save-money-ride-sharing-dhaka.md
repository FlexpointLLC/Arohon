For many families in Dhaka, transport is now one of the biggest monthly costs after rent and food. Small choices add up: one wrong vehicle type, one trip at the worst hour, one bargained CNG fare that went badly. Here are twelve ways to cut what you spend on getting around, without making every trip miserable.

## 1. Match the vehicle to the trip

The biggest saving is simply choosing the right vehicle.

| Trip | Usually cheapest choice |
|---|---|
| Solo, short to medium, light bag | Bike or EV bike |
| Two or three people, short trip | CNG |
| Family, luggage or rain | Car |
| Group of six or more | Micro or Hiace |

A bike is almost always the cheapest option for one person. Booking a car for a solo ten minute trip is where a lot of money quietly goes.

## 2. Know the fare before you book

Bargaining costs money when you are tired, late or new to an area. Apps with upfront fares remove that guesswork. On Arohon, the fare is shown before you book and there is no bargaining at pickup, so you can decide calmly whether the trip is worth it.

## 3. Compare at least two quotes

Different apps price the same trip differently, and the cheapest one changes by time and area. Keep two apps on your phone and check both for longer trips. Our guide to the cheapest ride sharing app in Bangladesh compares the main options.

## 4. Avoid the worst hours

Morning office time and the evening rush are when traffic is slowest and demand is highest. If your work allows it, leaving 30 minutes earlier or later can cut both travel time and cost. Apps that use time based pricing charge more for every minute you sit in a jam.

## 5. Watch out for surge

Some apps raise prices when demand spikes, such as in rain, after big events or before Eid. If you see a much higher fare than usual, wait a few minutes or switch to a different vehicle type. Read how surge pricing works on ride apps in Bangladesh before your next rainy day.

## 6. Use the metro rail for long north to south trips

For trips along the Uttara to Motijheel line, the metro is hard to beat. Fares are set at ৳5 per km, with a minimum of ৳20 and a maximum of ৳100 for the full run (Prothom Alo, Daily Star). Pair it with a short bike ride to and from the station and you often pay less than a long car trip while saving time.

## 7. Combine trips

If you need to visit the bank, the pharmacy and a relative in the same area, plan one route instead of three separate rides. Waiting a little at each stop is usually cheaper than three new pickups.

## 8. Order medicine and food instead of making a trip

A trip to the pharmacy and back can cost more than the delivery. Arohon medicine and food orders accept cash, bKash, Nagad and card, so you can skip the ride entirely for small errands.

## 9. Send a parcel, not a person

If you only need to drop off documents or a gift, a parcel is cheaper than a round trip. Arohon parcel delivers within 1 hour in Dhaka and to all 64 districts in 1 to 3 days, for items up to 8 kg.

## 10. Share bigger vehicles for long trips

For Eid or a family trip out of Dhaka, splitting a micro or Hiace among several people often costs less per person than individual bus tickets plus rides to and from the terminal. On Arohon rental, drivers send offers and you pick by price, rating and car, so you can compare before you commit.

Rides on Arohon are cash only. If you only have a large note, you may end up rounding up or waiting for change. Keeping ৳20, ৳50 and ৳100 notes makes every trip quicker and avoids losing small amounts each time.

## A sample monthly saving plan

Here is how a regular office commuter could think about it.

| Habit | What changes |
|---|---|
| Bike instead of car for solo trips | Lower fare on most daily rides |
| Leave 30 minutes earlier | Less time in traffic, fewer peak trips |
| Metro for long trips on the line | Fixed fare of ৳20 to ৳100 |
