## CNG fare in Sylhet: how to avoid overpaying

CNG auto rickshaws are everywhere in Sylhet, from Zindabazar to Amberkhana and the bus terminals. Without a working meter, the fare is a negotiation. A few habits help.

1. **Ask your hotel first.** Staff know the normal fare for common routes.
2. **Agree before you sit.** Confirm the amount and whether it is for the full vehicle or a shared seat.
3. **Compare with an app quote.** Even if you take the CNG, a bike or car quote on your phone gives you a reference price.
4. **Avoid peak times for long trips.** Office hours and evenings around the city centre are slower and pricier.

This is exactly the problem upfront pricing solves. On Arohon, you see the fare before you book, so there is no bargaining at the roadside.

## Osmani Airport to Sylhet city

Osmani International Airport sits on the northern side of Sylhet. The official Khadimnagar union portal puts it about 6 km from the city centre's zero point, though travel sites quote between 6 and 9 km depending on where in the city you are going.

## Planning a Sylhet tour by car

Most visitors want to see Ratargul, Bisnakandi, Jaflong, Lalakhal or the tea estates of Sreemangal. These are full day trips, and public transport to them is slow. A rented car or micro with a driver is the most common way to do them.

When you rent for a tour, ask these questions:

1. **Is fuel included?** Most day packages include it, but confirm.
2. **What are the hours?** A full day is often 8 to 10 hours. Extra time is charged.
3. **Are tolls and parking extra?** Usually yes.
4. **Where does the driver eat and stay?** On overnight trips, you may need to cover this.

## Safety tips for getting around Sylhet

1. **Use booked rides at night.** Especially from the bus terminal or railway station.
2. **Check the plate.** Make sure the vehicle matches what the app shows.
3. **Share your trip.** Live trip sharing lets family follow your route.
4. **Carry small notes.** Rides on Arohon are cash only, and many drivers cannot break large notes.
5. **Plan around the weather.** Monsoon rain can flood low roads and slow the haor and hill routes.
