Eid holidays end, offices reopen on the same day, and suddenly everyone is coming back to Dhaka at once. Buses arrive at Gabtoli and Sayedabad late at night, trains pull into Kamalapur packed, and the last stretch home becomes a fight for a CNG at whatever price the driver names. After a long journey with bags and tired children, that final hour can feel like the worst part of the trip.

With a bit of planning, the return can be calm. This guide covers when to travel, how to handle luggage, and how to get from the terminal to your door at a fair price.

## Why the return rush is so intense

Most people take the same holiday days, so the return is squeezed into the last one or two days before work resumes. Highways into Dhaka slow down, and when buses finally arrive, thousands of passengers need local transport at the same time. Street drivers know demand is high, and fares from terminals to areas like Mirpur, Uttara or Gulshan often jump.

## Pick the right day to come back

Your best tool is timing.

1. Return a day or two before work starts, if you can.
2. If you cannot, travel early in the morning rather than in the evening.
3. Avoid the last evening before offices reopen, which is usually the most crowded.

Some families split up: one adult returns first for work, while others stay a few extra days and travel when roads are clearer.

## The last mile: terminal to home

If you arrive by bus or train, plan the last stretch before you arrive.

1. Check your arrival time and keep your phone charged.
2. Walk a little away from the busiest exit before booking, so your driver can reach you.
3. Choose a vehicle that fits your luggage: a bike for one person with a backpack, a CNG or car for families with bags.
4. Confirm the driver's name, photo and plate before getting in.

An app with upfront fares means you see the price before you book, so you do not have to bargain at the terminal gate after a twelve hour journey.

## Safety tips for the return trip

1. Avoid unknown vehicles offered by strangers at terminals.
2. Keep valuables in a bag you hold yourself.
3. Share your trip details with family until you are home.
4. If you arrive very late, choose a car rather than a bike, especially with children.
