Most rides in Bangladesh still end the same way: the passenger hands the driver cash. But mobile wallets like bKash and Nagad are now part of daily life, and many passengers ask whether they can pay for a ride the same way they pay for groceries. Here is what each option looks like in 2026, and exactly how payment works on Arohon.

## The short answer for Arohon

**Arohon rides are cash only.** You pay the driver in cash at the end of the trip. There is no bKash, Nagad or card option for rides.

For **food and medicine orders**, Arohon accepts **cash, bKash, Nagad and card**.

| Arohon service | Cash | bKash | Nagad | Card |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rides (bike, EV bike, CNG, car, micro, Hiace and more) | Yes | No | No | No |
| Food | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Medicine | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

Because the fare is shown upfront before you book, you know the exact amount to have ready. No bargaining, no guessing.

## Why rides on Arohon are cash

Cash is simple for both sides:

1. **The driver gets paid at once.** No waiting for a weekly payout, no withdrawal charges.
2. **No balance problems.** A failed wallet payment at drop off is a bad end to any trip.
3. **Everyone has it.** Not every passenger has a wallet with balance, but almost everyone carries some cash.

Arohon drivers also pay a low flat fee to the platform, a 2% commission plus a ৳3 safety charge and a small booking fee, so being paid in cash directly keeps their income clear and immediate.

## Can you pay Uber with bKash?

Yes, Uber and bKash announced a partnership in August 2020 that let riders link a bKash account in the Uber app and pay for trips automatically (The Daily Star and TBS, 2020). At launch it covered Dhaka, Chattogram, Sylhet and Cox's Bazar. Uber's own blog said the bKash account had to match the phone number registered on Uber. Check the payment menu in the app for what is available to you today.

## What is Pathao Pay?

Pathao Pay is Pathao's own digital wallet. Bangladesh Bank granted Pathao's subsidiary a payment service provider licence in April 2023 (The Daily Star, 2023), and the wallet was publicly launched in July 2025. Which Pathao services it covers and any fees are best checked inside the Pathao app.

## Paying with Nagad

Nagad is widely used for bill payments, shopping and sending money. For rides, support depends on the app. On Arohon, Nagad is accepted for food and medicine, not for rides.

## Cash, wallet or card: pros and cons

| Method | Good for | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Cash | Works everywhere, driver paid at once | Keep small notes, change can be a problem |
| bKash | Fast, no change needed | Needs balance and data at the end of the trip |
| Nagad | Popular for bills and shopping | Not every app supports it for rides |
| Card | Useful for people who do not carry cash | Fewer people in Bangladesh use cards daily |

## Tips for paying cash smoothly

1. **Check the upfront fare** before you confirm, and keep that amount ready.
2. **Carry small notes.** ৳10, ৳20 and ৳50 notes save time at drop off.
3. **Pay at the destination,** not before the trip starts.
4. **Count your change** before the driver leaves.
5. **Do not hand over more than the shown fare.** On Arohon the fare is agreed before you book, so there is nothing to negotiate.

## Ordering food or medicine with bKash

When you order food or medicine on Arohon, you can choose cash on delivery or pay with bKash, Nagad or card. Digital payment is handy for late night medicine orders, when you may not have the right change at home.

## A note on safety

Whatever you pay with, the trip should feel safe. On Arohon, you see the driver's name, photo and plate before pickup and can share your live trip with family. Never share your wallet PIN or a one time code with anyone, including a driver or someone claiming to be support.
