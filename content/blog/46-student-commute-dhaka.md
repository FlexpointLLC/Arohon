For most students in Dhaka, transport is the second biggest cost after food. A daily trip from Mirpur to Dhaka University or from Uttara to a private university in Bashundhara can eat a large part of a monthly allowance. The good news is that you have more options than before. Here is how they compare, using published fares only.

## Your options at a glance

| Option | Typical cost | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| City bus with student half fare | Lowest | Daily trips on main roads |
| Metro rail | Fixed fare by distance | Trips along the MRT Line 6 corridor |
| Bike ride on an app | Higher, but fast | Late for class, short gaps, exam days |
| CNG | Higher, carries more | Two or three friends sharing |
| Walking or rickshaw | Low for short trips | Last few hundred metres |

The cheapest plan for most students is a mix: bus or metro for the long part, and a ride only when time matters.

## City bus and the student half fare

The bus is still the cheapest way across Dhaka.

1. **The rate.** BRTA set city bus fares in Dhaka at ৳2.45 per km in 2022, and reports in 2024 said this was cut to ৳2.42, though some reporting says the cut was not applied. The minimum fare is ৳10 on buses and ৳8 on minibuses.
2. **Half fare for students.** After student protests in late 2021, bus owners in Dhaka agreed to charge students half fare on city buses (TBS and The Daily Star, 2021). Later reports said owners announced half fare for students seven days a week on metro area buses.
3. **The conditions.** Reports say the half fare applies from 7 am to 8 pm, you must show your student ID, and it does not apply on government holidays or your institution's holidays.

Always carry your ID card. Without it, many conductors will not give the half fare, and arguments on the bus are common, as passenger groups have pointed out.

## Metro rail

If your route runs along MRT Line 6 between Uttara and Motijheel, the metro is often the fastest option, with no traffic at all.

| Metro trip | Fare |
|---|---|
| Minimum fare | ৳20 |
| Agargaon to Motijheel | ৳50 |
| Uttara to Farmgate | ৳70 |
| Uttara to Motijheel | ৳100 |

The fare was set at ৳5 per km with a ৳20 minimum (The Daily Star, Prothom Alo, 2022). For Dhaka University students, stations near Shahbag and TSC make the metro useful. Check current fares at the station before you plan a monthly budget.

## Example: Dhaka University to Mirpur

This is one of the most common student routes. A typical plan:

1. **Morning, no rush:** take the metro from Mirpur 10 or Kazipara towards Shahbag or Dhaka University station, then walk.
2. **Late for class:** book a bike ride for the whole trip, or ride to the nearest metro station.
3. **Evening with friends:** share a CNG with two classmates going the same way.

## When a bike ride makes sense for students

A bike ride costs more than a bus seat, but there are times it is worth it:

1. **Exams and viva.** Arriving late can cost far more than the fare.
2. **Gaps between classes and tuition.** A quick ride lets you fit a tuition in the evening.
3. **Late at night.** When buses thin out, a tracked ride is safer than waiting on the road.

## Splitting the cost

1. **Share a CNG or car** with friends from the same area. Split the upfront fare.
2. **Plan a group ride home** after evening classes or events.
3. **Refer your friends.** On Arohon, passengers earn ৳10 cash for each friend they refer. In a big class group, that adds up.

## Simple money saving habits

1. **Leave 15 minutes earlier** and take the bus instead of paying for a rush ride.
2. **Use the metro for long north to south trips.**
3. **Keep small notes** for buses and cash rides.
4. **Track a week of spending** on transport. Most students are surprised by the total.
5. **Avoid peak hours** when you can. Traffic, not distance, is what costs you time.

### What is the cheapest way to get to university in Dhaka?

A city bus with the student half fare is usually the cheapest. The metro is often the fastest on the Uttara to Motijheel corridor.
