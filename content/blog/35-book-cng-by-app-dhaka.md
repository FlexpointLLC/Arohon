Ask anyone in Dhaka about CNG auto rickshaws and you will hear the same story. The driver refuses the meter, names a price far above what it should be, and you either argue or give in. Booking a CNG by app solves most of that. You see the fare before you book, the driver comes to you, and there is nothing to negotiate. This guide explains how it works.

## Why street CNG fares feel unfair

Dhaka's CNGs are supposed to run on meter. The official rate set in 2015 was ৳40 for the first 2 km and ৳12 for each km after that. In practice, many drivers ignore the meter and quote a contract price. Our [CNG meter complaint guide](https://blogs.arohon.co/cng-meter-complaint-dhaka) covers how to report this, but most people simply want a fair price without a fight.

A Policy Research Institute estimate put a typical CNG trip in Dhaka at around ৳250. Street quotes often go well above the meter, especially during rain, rush hour and Eid.

## How to book a CNG on Arohon, step by step

1. **Open the app** and enter your pickup point. A main road is easiest for the driver.
2. **Enter your destination.**
3. **Choose CNG** from the ride types.
4. **See the fare.** The app shows the exact fare before you book. This is the price you pay.
5. **Confirm the booking.** You will see the driver's name, photo and number plate.
6. **Check the plate** when the CNG arrives.
7. **Share your trip** with family if you want someone to follow along.
8. **Pay in cash** at the end. Rides on Arohon are cash only.

No bargaining at the door, no "meter kharap" and no surprise at the end.

## Is an app CNG cheaper than the street?

Not always cheaper than the official meter, but usually cheaper than a street contract price, and always more predictable. The real saving is time and stress. You know the cost before you leave home.

## When a CNG is the right choice

1. **Two or three passengers.** A CNG seats more people than a bike for a similar trip.
2. **Rainy days.** You stay drier than on a bike.
3. **Shopping or luggage.** There is room for bags.
4. **Night rides.** A closed seat feels safer than a bike for many passengers.
5. **Medium distances.** Too far to walk, not worth a car.

## Tips for a smooth app CNG ride

1. **Set an exact pickup pin.** Dhaka lanes are confusing, so drop the pin on a landmark.
2. **Call if needed.** A quick call helps the driver find you.
3. **Keep small notes.** Exact cash speeds up the end of the trip.
4. **Do not agree to changes.** If a driver asks for more than the app fare, you do not have to pay it. Report it in the app.
5. **Check the door latch.** Make sure the side grill or door closes properly before you set off.
