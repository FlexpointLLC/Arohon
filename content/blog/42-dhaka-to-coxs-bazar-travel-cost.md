Cox's Bazar is still the trip most Bangladeshi families plan at least once a year. The beach is the easy part. The hard part is choosing how to get there, because bus, train, flight and private car all cost very different amounts and suit different groups. This guide compares the four with published prices, so you can pick before you book.

Bus and flight fares change by date and season. Train fares have been reported differently by different outlets since launch, so always check the current price when you book.

## By bus

Buses are still the most common way to go. Many operators run overnight services from Dhaka, including Green Line, Shyamoli and others listed on booking platforms like Shohoz and bdtickets.

| Bus class | Reported fare |
|---|---|
| Non AC | About ৳1,100 |
| AC | About ৳2,000 |
| Sleeper | Higher than AC; check the operator |

**Good for:** solo travellers and couples on a budget.
**Watch out for:** Eid and winter holiday rush, when tickets sell out fast. Read our guide on what to do when Eid bus tickets are sold out.

Remember to budget the ride to the bus counter at Sayedabad, Arambagh, Kalabagan or wherever your operator departs from.

## By train

The Cox's Bazar Express started running on the Dhaka to Cox's Bazar route in December 2023, and more trains were added later. At launch, these fares were reported:

| Class | Launch fare |
|---|---|
| Shovon chair | ৳695 |
| AC chair | ৳1,325 |
| AC seat | ৳1,590 |
| AC berth | ৳2,380 |

Some outlets reported different figures at the time, and later reports show other numbers, so check the Bangladesh Railway ticketing site for the current price. Tickets are popular and often sell out within hours of release.

**Good for:** travellers who want a comfortable overnight trip at a lower price than AC buses.
**Watch out for:** tickets selling out, and getting to Kamalapur or Airport station early.

## By flight

Biman, US Bangla and Novoair fly between Dhaka and Cox's Bazar. ShareTrip's fare guide lists one way fares starting around ৳4,000 on Biman and around ৳4,500 on US Bangla and Novoair. Prices rise on weekends and holidays.

**Good for:** short trips, business travellers, anyone short on time.
**Remember:** add the ride to Shahjalal Airport. Our airport guides cover timing and fares.

## By private car or micro

For families and groups, a private car or micro is often the most comfortable and, once split, competitive per person. Rental company price lists in 2026 put a Dhaka to Cox's Bazar drop at around ৳15,000 for a private car. Micros and Hiace vans cost more per vehicle, but carry more people.

| Group size | Usual vehicle | How the cost works |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 3 | Car | One fare split by up to 3 |
| 4 | Car or Car Plus | Bigger car for luggage |
| 6 to 10 | Micro | One fare split by the group |
| 10 and above | Hiace | One fare split by a larger group |

**Good for:** families with children, elderly parents, groups of friends, lots of luggage.
**Ask before you book:** is fuel included, are tolls and driver food extra, and what does a return trip or waiting days cost.
