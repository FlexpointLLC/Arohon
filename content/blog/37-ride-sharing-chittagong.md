Chittagong, officially Chattogram, is the second largest city in Bangladesh and the country's main port. Its hills, long roads like the CDA Avenue and busy areas such as Agrabad, GEC and Nasirabad make getting around a mix of bike, CNG and car. Ride sharing apps have been in the city for years, though the line up has changed. Here is what we could verify for 2026.

## Getting a CNG in Chittagong

CNGs are everywhere in Chattogram, but as in Dhaka, many drivers prefer a contract price to the meter. A few habits help:

1. **Know the rough distance.** A map app gives you the km, so you can judge whether a quote is fair.
2. **Agree before you sit.** If you take a street CNG, settle the price first, not at the destination.
3. **Use an app where you can.** An app booking gives you a fixed fare and the driver's details.
4. **Avoid the busiest stands.** Drivers at the railway station, bus terminals and the port area often quote higher.

Our [CNG fare guide](https://blogs.arohon.co/cng-fare-dhaka) covers how meter fares work in Dhaka, which helps you judge prices anywhere.

## Bike rides in Chittagong

Bikes are fast through traffic around Agrabad, Muradpur and 2 No Gate. The hills in areas like Khulshi and the slopes near Batali Hill are fine on a bike, but wet roads in the monsoon can be slippery. Wear the helmet, check the plate and avoid bikes in heavy rain.

## Safety tips for any app in Chittagong

1. **Match the plate and photo** before you get in.
2. **Share your trip** with family, especially at night.
3. **Keep your phone charged** and data on.
4. **Pick a lit pickup point** on a main road.
5. **Carry cash in small notes** for cash rides.
