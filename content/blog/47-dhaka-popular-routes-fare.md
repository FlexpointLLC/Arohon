"Bhara koto?" is the first question on any Dhaka trip. The answer depends on the vehicle, the time of day and whether anyone follows the official rate. This guide gives estimated fares on popular routes, worked out from published government rates, so you have a fair reference before you travel.

## How we worked out these numbers

We did not invent any fare. For each route we:

1. **Took an approximate road distance from a map.** Real distance depends on the exact start and end point and the road the driver takes.
2. **Multiplied by the published rate** for that vehicle.
3. **Rounded the result** and labelled it as an estimate.

The published rates we used:

| Vehicle | Published rate | Source |
|---|---|---|
| City bus | ৳2.42 to ৳2.45 per km, minimum ৳10 | BRTA rates reported in 2022 and 2024 |
| CNG auto rickshaw | ৳40 for the first 2 km, then ৳12 per km, ৳2 per minute waiting | Government rate from 2015 |
| Metro rail | ৳5 per km, minimum ৳20 | Government fare, 2022 |

One honest warning: reporting by TBS in 2022 said the official CNG meter rate has not been implemented for years, and drivers often charge by bargaining. The CNG figures below show what the official rate works out to, not what you will always be asked to pay.

Waiting time in traffic adds ৳2 per minute on a metered CNG, so a slow trip will cost more than the table shows.

## Route by route tips

**Mirpur to Motijheel.** The metro beats traffic on most weekdays. If you need a door to door ride, a bike is usually quicker than a car at peak hour.

**Uttara to Gulshan.** Airport Road and Banani traffic can double the travel time in the evening. Leave before 5 pm or after 8 pm if you can.

**Mirpur to Dhanmondi.** Short on the map but slow through Mirpur Road. A bike ride often saves the most time here.

**Uttara to Motijheel.** The metro covers the full stretch for ৳100 and avoids the worst traffic in the city.

## How to avoid overpaying

1. **Know the rough fare** before you step out. Use the estimates above as a reference.
2. **Use an app with upfront fares** so the price is fixed before you start.
3. **Avoid peak hours** when you can. Waiting charges and surge pricing cost more than distance.
4. **Share with friends** going the same way and split the fare.
5. **Check the driver** before you get in. On Arohon, the driver's name, photo and plate show before pickup, and you can share your live trip with family.
