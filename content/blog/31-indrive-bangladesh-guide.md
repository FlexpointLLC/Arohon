inDrive works differently from every other ride app in Dhaka. Instead of the app setting the price, you propose a fare and drivers decide whether to accept it or offer their own. Some passengers love the control. Others find it slow and confusing. This guide explains how inDrive works in Bangladesh, its real strengths, its weak points, and how it compares with Pathao.

## How inDrive works

1. **Enter your pickup and drop off.** The app suggests a recommended fare.
2. **Set your offer.** You can keep the suggestion, raise it or lower it.
3. **Drivers respond.** Nearby drivers see your request and can accept your price or counter with a higher one.
4. **You choose.** You see each driver's offer, rating, vehicle and arrival time, then pick one.
5. **Pay at the end.** You pay the agreed amount to the driver.

inDrive describes this as a fair deal for both sides: passengers propose and drivers can counter. It is a bidding model, not a fixed rate card.

## inDrive fare in Dhaka: what does it cost?

There is no published inDrive rate card for Bangladesh, because the price is whatever you and the driver agree. The suggested fare in the app is a starting point, not a rule.

In practice, your final price depends on:

1. **Time of day.** At rush hour drivers often counter higher.
2. **Your offer.** A very low offer may get no response.
3. **The route.** Drivers may avoid trips into heavy traffic or areas where they will struggle to find a return trip.
4. **Vehicle type.** Bike, car and other options are priced separately.

For a sense of a fair price, use benchmarks. Rest of World reported in 2022 that Pathao's bike fare was ৳25 base plus ৳12 per km, later raised to ৳15 per km in Dhaka (The Daily Star, 2022). If your offer is far below that kind of figure, expect a long wait.

## Pros of inDrive

1. **You control the price.** On quiet routes at quiet hours, you can often pay less.
2. **You choose the driver.** You can pick based on rating, vehicle and arrival time, not just the first match.
3. **Transparency on the deal.** You agree the fare before the trip starts.
4. **Drivers like the flexibility.** Some drivers prefer seeing the price before they accept, which can mean fewer cancellations once accepted.

## Cons of inDrive

1. **It takes time.** Bidding back and forth can take several minutes, which matters when you are late.
2. **You need to know a fair price.** On an unfamiliar route, it is easy to offer too much or too little.
3. **Rush hour erases the saving.** When demand is high, you may raise your offer several times.
4. **Unclear rules.** The Business Standard reported in 2023 that no official information on inDrive's commission policy in Bangladesh was available, and that inDrive was not on the list of BRTA approved ride sharing platforms at the time. This may have changed, so check the current status.

## Is inDrive safe?

inDrive shows the driver's details and vehicle before you accept and has in app safety features, but as with any app, your own checks matter most:

1. **Match the plate and photo** before you get in.
2. **Share your trip** with family or a friend.
3. **Sit in the back** of a car, and wear the helmet on a bike.
4. **Agree the fare in the app**, never change it in cash at the end.
5. **Avoid unlit, empty pickup points** at night.

## inDrive vs Pathao

| | inDrive | Pathao |
|---|---|---|
| How the price is set | You offer, drivers accept or counter | Rate card with peak pricing |
| Speed of booking | Slower, depends on bidding | Usually fast matching |
| Best for | Patient passengers on known routes | Quick bike trips across Dhaka |
| Extra services | Mainly rides | Rides, food and parcel |
| Price certainty | Agreed before trip | Estimate shown before booking |

Neither is always cheaper. inDrive can win at quiet hours when you know the route. Pathao is usually quicker when you just need to go.

## Bidding vs a fixed upfront fare

Bidding gives control but asks you to negotiate on every trip. A fixed upfront fare gives certainty with no negotiation. ## Frequently asked questions

### How does inDrive work in Bangladesh?

You enter your trip and offer a fare. Nearby drivers accept it or counter with their own price. You pick the driver you want and pay the agreed amount.

### Is inDrive cheaper than Pathao?

It can be at quiet hours on routes you know, because you set the offer. At rush hour you may need to raise it, and Pathao's rate card can work out similar or cheaper.

### Is inDrive safe in Dhaka?

inDrive shows driver and vehicle details before you accept. Always match the plate and photo, share your trip and avoid empty pickup spots at night.

### Why is no driver accepting my inDrive offer?

Your offer is probably below what drivers expect for that route and time. Raise it a little or wait until traffic eases.

### How much commission does inDrive take in Bangladesh?

The Business Standard reported in 2023 that no official commission information for inDrive in Bangladesh was available. Check the inDrive driver app for current terms.
