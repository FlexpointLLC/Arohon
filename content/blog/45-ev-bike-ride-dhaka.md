Stand at any Dhaka signal and you will hear horns, engines and exhaust. Now and then a bike rolls past almost silently. Electric bikes are still a small share of the traffic, but interest is growing among commuters, delivery riders and ride sharing drivers. Here is what we know, from published sources, about owning one and riding on one in Bangladesh.

## What counts as an EV bike

An EV bike is a motorbike or scooter that runs on a battery and an electric motor instead of petrol. You charge it from a power point rather than filling it at a pump. In Bangladesh you will see two broad kinds:

1. **Low speed electric scooters**, often used for short local trips.
2. **Registrable electric motorcycles**, which can be registered with BRTA and used on city roads like any bike.

On Arohon, **EV bike is one of the vehicle options** you can choose when you book, alongside bike, CNG, car and more.

## What does it cost to run?

The main saving is fuel. A TBS report estimated that charging an electric bike costs only about ৳300 to ৳500 a month. Walton listed a charging time of 6 to 8 hours for its Takyon scooter, so most owners charge overnight at home.

Compare that with filling a petrol bike every few days for daily riding, and the difference over a year is real. Electric bikes also have fewer moving parts, so there is no engine oil to change.

The catch, as The Daily Star has reported, is that access to charging and rising electricity prices have held back adoption. If you live in a building without a safe place to charge, plan that first.

## Registration and rules

1. **BRTA registration.** BRTA issued a policy for registering electric vehicles in 2020, according to The Daily Star. Dealer listings say registrable models need BRTA registration, and they quote estimated costs, not official fees.
2. **Few are registered so far.** A TBS report said official BRTA data showed only 261 electric two wheel vehicles among about 6.5 million registered vehicles, while thousands more run unregistered.
3. **Tax support.** TBS also reported that duties on local production have been cut and VAT and advance tax on imported parts waived, which helps local makers.

If you plan to ride for a ride sharing app, use a registered bike with valid papers.

## Is an EV bike ride better for passengers?

From the passenger seat, a few things stand out:

1. **Quieter.** No engine noise and no exhaust smell at signals.
2. **Smoother start.** Electric motors pull away gently, which many passengers find comfortable.
3. **Cleaner air.** No tailpipe emissions on the street where you ride.

## Should drivers switch to electric?

For a ride sharing driver, lower running cost can mean more profit per trip. Things to weigh:

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Range per charge | Enough for a full shift without stopping? |
| Charging at home | Safe wiring and space to charge overnight? |
| Registration | Can you get BRTA papers for this model? |
| Service and spare parts | Is there a service centre near you? |
| Battery life | Ask the dealer about warranty and replacement cost |

Whatever you ride, what you keep per trip matters. On Arohon, drivers pay a flat 2% commission plus a ৳3 safety charge and a small booking fee, so on a ৳100 trip the driver keeps ৳90. Lower fuel cost plus lower commission adds up.

## Riding safely

Electric bikes are quiet, which means pedestrians may not hear them coming. Drivers should use the horn sensibly and slow down near crossings. Passengers should always wear the helmet, on any bike.
