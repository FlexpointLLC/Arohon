Few things are more stressful than a missed flight or being swarmed by drivers at arrivals after a long journey. A little planning makes Dhaka's airport run easy, whether you are flying out at 5 AM or landing at midnight.

## Going to the airport

Traffic around Banani, Mohakhali and the airport road is unpredictable. Leave more time than you think.

| From | Typical drive outside peak hours | Leave this early before an international flight |
|---|---|---|
| Uttara | 15 to 30 min | 3.5 hours |
| Gulshan or Banani | 30 to 45 min | 4 hours |
| Dhanmondi or Mohammadpur | 45 to 75 min | 4.5 hours |
| Old Dhaka or Motijheel | 60 to 90 min | 5 hours |

These are rough guides. In heavy traffic or rain, trips can take much longer.

## Coming home from the airport

Arrivals can be chaotic, with drivers offering rides and prices that change once you are in the car. The calm way is simple: **book before you land** so your driver is confirmed before you collect your bags.

- Agree the pickup point in advance
- Keep your phone charged and roaming or Wi Fi ready
- Check the car and plate before you get in
- Share your trip with family

## FAQ

**How early should I leave for Dhaka airport?**
For international flights, plan to arrive 3 hours before departure, and add your travel time on top. From Gulshan that usually means leaving about 4 hours before.

**How do I book a car from Dhaka airport?**
Book in the Arohon app before you land. Drivers send offers, and your ride is confirmed before you arrive.

**Can I book for a family with lots of luggage?**
Yes. Choose a micro for up to 7 people or a Hiace for up to 12.
