Ask anyone in Dhaka about CNG fares and you will hear the same story: "The meter says one thing, the driver says another." This guide explains the official rate, why it rarely applies on the street, and the simplest way to pay a fair price every time.

## The official CNG meter rate

The government sets a meter fare for CNG autorickshaws:

| Part | Official rate |
|---|---|
| First 2 km | ৳40 |
| Every km after | ৳12 |
| Waiting | ৳2 per minute |

BRTA rules say drivers who charge above the meter can face a fine of up to ৳50,000 or up to six months in prison.

## What people actually pay

In practice, very few CNG trips in Dhaka run on the meter. Passengers regularly report that even a short trip rarely costs less than ৳150 when negotiated on the street. Drivers point to high daily rental fees paid to vehicle owners, fuel costs and traffic.

The result is the daily bargaining most of us know too well: you name a price, the driver names a higher one, and you either give in or wait for the next CNG.

## The app booked alternative

Booking a CNG through an app solves the bargaining problem. The fare is calculated from distance and time and shown before you book. On Arohon, a CNG is priced at:

| Part | Rate |
|---|---|
| Base fare | ৳70 |
| Per km | ৳14 |
| Per minute | ৳1.50 |
| Minimum fare | ৳80 |

Plus a **৳3 safety charge** and a small **booking fee** (৳5 below ৳200, ৳10 from ৳200 to ৳349).

## CNG or bike?

- **One person, light bag:** a bike is cheaper and faster.
- **Two or three people:** a CNG often costs less per person than separate bikes.
- **Rain, luggage, or a child with you:** CNG every time.

## Tips for a fair CNG ride

- **Book in the app** so the price is fixed before the trip starts.
- **Check the plate** matches the one in the app before you get in.
- **Share your trip** with family if you are travelling at night.
- **Pay cash at the end**, at the fare you saw. No extra.
