Pathao is the app that made bike rides normal in Dhaka, and "Pathao bhara koto?" is still one of the most common questions passengers ask. The trouble is that most answers online are from 2018. This guide pulls together the published Pathao rates we could verify, with their dates, explains how the fare is built, and shows how to estimate your own trip.

## How a Pathao fare is calculated

A Pathao ride is priced from:

1. **Base fare**, charged once per trip
2. **Per km rate**, for distance
3. **Per minute rate**, for time on the road
4. **Peak pricing**, a higher rate when demand is high

The app shows you an estimated fare before you confirm.

## Pathao car fare per km

Pathao has not published its current car rate card in a source we could verify. Car fares are higher than bike fares because of fuel, vehicle cost and more space. Under the Ride Sharing Service Policy 2017, ride share fares may not exceed taxi cab fares. The Taxicab Service Guidelines 2010 set a cab fare of ৳85 for the first 2 km and ৳34 for each km after that, which works as an outer ceiling. For the real price of your trip, enter it in the Pathao app.

## How Pathao peak pricing works

When requests outnumber available drivers, Pathao charges more. Peak pricing is most common during office rush, rain and holidays.

Commission is a separate matter that affects drivers. The Business Standard reported in 2021 that Pathao charged drivers 10% commission at peak times and up to 15% off peak. Many drivers have since asked for lower cuts, and some prefer offline trips for that reason.

## Is there a Pathao fare calculator?

The Pathao app is the calculator. Put in your pickup and drop off, choose bike or car, and you see the fare before you book. Online Pathao calculators often still use 2018 rates, so their numbers can be far off. If you want a quick mental estimate for a bike, use the formula above and add some margin for traffic.

## Where Pathao does well

1. **Huge bike supply.** In most parts of Dhaka you can find a bike in minutes.
2. **One app for many things.** Rides, food and parcel in the same app.
3. **Digital payment options** for many services.
4. **Brand recognition.** Most drivers and passengers know how it works.

## Tips to pay less on Pathao

1. **Avoid the rush.** Book before 8 am or after 8 pm if you can.
2. **Wait out short spikes.** Peak prices can drop within 10 to 15 minutes.
3. **Pick a main road pickup.** It reduces detours and waiting time.
4. **Choose the right vehicle.** Solo with no bag? A bike is cheaper than a car.
5. **Compare live prices** across two or three apps before you confirm.
