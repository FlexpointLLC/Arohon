Forgot your laptop charger at home? Sending a birthday gift across town? Shipping an order to a customer in Rangpur? Bangladesh has more than 700 courier companies, but only a fraction are licensed, and most do not let you track your parcel live. Here is how to send parcels the easy way.

## Delivery options

| Option | When it arrives | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Quick Delivery** | Within about 1 hour | Documents, keys, chargers across the city |
| **4 Hour Delivery** | The same afternoon | Gifts, clothes, office packages |
| **Same Day Delivery** | By tonight | Longer city runs |
| **Nationwide** | 1 to 3 days | Any of Bangladesh's 64 districts |

The exact price and expected time are shown in the app before you confirm.

## What you can send

Documents, food, homemade food, clothes, gifts, cosmetics, medicine, accessories, electronics and most everyday items, **up to 8 kg per parcel**. Heavier items can be split into smaller parcels, or sent with a pickup or truck instead.

## What you cannot send

- Passports and official identity documents
- Bank cheques, cash and jewellery
- Illegal or prohibited drugs
- Weapons, fireworks, anything flammable or toxic
- Live animals
- Anything leaking or dangerously packed

## How to pack it right

- **Seal food** in containers that will not leak if tipped.
- **Wrap fragile items** with bubble wrap or cloth and fill empty space.
- **Use a sturdy box or bag** a rider can carry safely on a bike.
- **Write the receiver's phone number** on the parcel as a backup.
- **Describe the contents honestly** in the app.

## FAQ

**How fast is parcel delivery in Dhaka?**
Quick Delivery usually arrives within an hour across the city. 4 Hour and Same Day options are also available.

**Can I send parcels outside Dhaka?**
Yes, to any of Bangladesh's 64 districts in 1 to 3 days.

**What is the weight limit?**
8 kg per parcel.

**Can I send medicine?**
