When someone you love is sick, every minute feels like an hour. This guide is written to be useful in that moment: what to do first, how to get an ambulance quickly in Dhaka, and how to prepare so the crew can help from the moment they arrive.

## First: is it life threatening?

If someone is unconscious, not breathing, has severe chest pain, heavy bleeding or signs of a stroke, **call 999 immediately**. 999 is Bangladesh's national emergency number. Then arrange transport while you wait for instructions.

## How to book an ambulance from your phone

Arohon lets you book an ambulance from the same app you ride with, any hour:

1. **Choose the hospital.** The app suggests hospitals nearest to you first, or you can search any place.
2. **Tell the crew what the patient needs**: a stretcher, oxygen, a wheelchair, or space for a companion.
3. **Pickup now or schedule.** Book for right away, or schedule a hospital visit later.
4. **Choose an offer.** Nearby ambulances send their price. Pick the one that suits you.
5. **Pay the agreed price in cash** to the crew at the end of the trip.

## What to tell the crew

The more the crew knows, the better prepared they arrive:

- Is the patient able to sit or walk?
- Do they need oxygen?
- Is there a lift in the building, or stairs?
- Will a family member travel along?
- Which hospital, and does the hospital know you are coming?

## Be ready before you need it

- Save **999** and your nearest hospital's emergency number in your phone.
- Keep a folder with the patient's prescriptions, reports and NID.
- Know your building's access: lift size, gate code, and the best pickup point.
- Install a booking app now, not during an emergency.

## FAQ

**What is the emergency number in Bangladesh?**
999, for police, fire and ambulance.

**How do I book an ambulance in Dhaka?**
Open the Arohon app, choose Ambulance, pick the hospital and needs, and nearby ambulances send you offers.

**Can a family member travel with the patient?**
Yes. Add a companion when you book.

**Can I book an ambulance for later?**
Yes. Schedule a pickup for a hospital visit at a set time.
