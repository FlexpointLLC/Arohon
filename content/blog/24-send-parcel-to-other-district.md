## What affects the cost?

Courier charges in Bangladesh generally depend on the same things, whichever service you use:

1. **Weight.** Heavier parcels cost more.
2. **Size.** A large light box can cost more than its weight suggests because it takes more space.
3. **Destination.** Inside Dhaka is usually cheaper than outside Dhaka.
4. **Cash on delivery.** Collecting money from the receiver may add a small charge.
5. **Fragile handling.** Glass and electronics need extra care and packing.

For the exact price of your parcel, check the Arohon app when you book.

## How to pack a parcel for a long trip

A parcel to another district changes hands and travels by road for hours. Pack it as if it will be dropped once.

1. **Use a strong box.** A fresh carton is better than a soft, used one.
2. **Wrap items separately.** Use bubble wrap, newspaper or cloth around each piece.
3. **Fill empty space.** Nothing should move when you shake the box.
4. **Seal every edge.** Tape the top, bottom and side seams.
5. **Waterproof it.** Put clothes, papers and books in a plastic bag first. Rain is common, especially in monsoon.
6. **Label clearly.** Write the receiver's full name, phone number, district, upazila and a landmark. Add your own number too.
7. **Mark fragile items.** Write "Fragile" and "This side up" on the box.

## What not to send

Do not send cash, explosives, gas cylinders, illegal items, or anything that leaks or spoils fast. Perishable food may not survive 1 to 3 days. If you are unsure, ask before booking.

## Cash on delivery tips for online sellers

Many Facebook and online shops sell across Bangladesh using cash on delivery. It builds trust with buyers, but returns and fake orders can hurt your profit. A few habits help:

1. **Call to confirm every order.** A quick call before dispatch filters out many fake or impulse orders.
2. **Write the full address.** Ask for a landmark and an alternate phone number, especially for orders outside district towns.
3. **Take a small advance on costly items.** Asking for the delivery charge in advance through bKash or Nagad reduces returns.
4. **Describe products honestly.** Real photos and correct sizes reduce "not as shown" returns.
5. **Pack with your brand.** A clean, labelled package makes a repeat order more likely.
6. **Track every parcel.** Keep a simple sheet of order, buyer, date sent and status, so you can follow up quickly.
7. **Dispatch early.** Send orders the same day where possible. Fast delivery means fewer cancellations.
