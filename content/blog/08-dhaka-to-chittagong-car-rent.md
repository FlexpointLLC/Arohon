## Car, Car Plus, micro or Hiace?

- **Car:** up to 4 people with normal luggage. Best value for couples and small families.
- **Car Plus:** roomier cars and top rated drivers for long drives and business trips.
- **Micro:** up to 7 people, ideal for families with grandparents and suitcases.
- **Hiace:** up to 12, perfect for office trips, weddings and tours.

## Tips for a smooth intercity trip

- **Book a day ahead** for holidays and weekends, especially around Eid.
- **Leave early** to beat traffic leaving Dhaka.
- **Keep cash for tolls** on bridges and expressways.
- **Plan a rest stop** every 3 to 4 hours for the driver.
- **Share your trip** with family so they can follow along.
