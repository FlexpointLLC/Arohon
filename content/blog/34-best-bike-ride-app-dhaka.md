For most people in Dhaka, a bike ride is the fastest way through traffic. It slips between buses, costs less than a car and gets you to class or office on time. But which app should you open? The answer depends on what you care about most: a fixed price, the lowest price, the fastest pickup or the driver's share of the fare. This comparison looks at the four main options in 2026.

## How bike ride apps price a trip

There are two main models in Dhaka.

1. **Fixed upfront fare.** The app calculates the price from distance and time and shows it before you book. Pathao, Uber and Arohon work this way.
2. **Offer and bargain.** You suggest a price and drivers accept, reject or counter. inDrive is built on this model.

A fixed fare is simpler and avoids negotiation. The bargaining model can be cheaper at quiet times, but it takes longer and the result depends on how hard you push.

## The four apps compared

### Pathao

Pathao is one of the best known bike ride brands in Bangladesh. You get a fixed fare shown in the app before you book. It also offers food, parcel and other services in the same app.

### Uber Moto

Uber added Moto in Bangladesh years ago and, according to a 2022 Daily Star report, expanded the service to 20 cities. Uber's strength is a familiar app used around the world. Bike availability depends heavily on your area.

### inDrive

inDrive lets you name your price. If you are patient and travel at off peak times, you may pay less. During rush hour, low offers are often ignored, and you may end up raising your price or waiting. Read our full [inDrive Bangladesh guide](https://blogs.arohon.co/indrive-bangladesh-guide).

### Arohon

Arohon offers both regular bike and EV bike rides. The app shows the exact fare before you book, so there is no bargaining at pickup. Rides are paid in cash. Before the driver arrives, you see their name, photo and number plate, and you can share your trip live with family.

What sets Arohon apart is the driver side. Drivers pay a flat 2% commission, a ৳3 safety charge and a small booking fee based on fare (৳5 under ৳200, ৳10 for ৳200 to ৳349, ৳15 for ৳350 to ৳499, ৳20 from ৳500). On a ৳100 trip, the driver keeps ৳90. When drivers keep more, they have less reason to cancel short trips or ask for extra money.

## What about EV bikes?

Electric bikes are quiet, have no exhaust smoke and are becoming more common in Dhaka. For a passenger, an EV bike ride feels much like a regular bike ride, just smoother. Arohon lists EV bike as a separate ride type, so you can choose it when booking. Range and charging are the driver's concern, not yours.

## Which bike app is cheapest?

There is no single winner. Fares change with demand, distance and time of day, and most apps do not publish their current rates. The simplest method is to check two apps before you book and compare the upfront price. ## Tips for a better bike ride in Dhaka

1. **Wear the helmet.** Ask for one if the driver does not offer it.
2. **Check the plate.** Make sure it matches the app before you sit.
3. **Avoid heavy rain.** Waterlogged roads make bikes risky. Take a CNG or car instead.
4. **Carry small notes.** Rides on Arohon are cash only, so exact change helps.
5. **Pick a clear pickup point.** A main road is easier for the driver to find than a narrow lane.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which is the best bike ride app in Dhaka?

It depends on your priority. Pathao has wide coverage, inDrive lets you bargain, and Arohon shows a fixed upfront fare with EV bike options and a flat 2% driver commission. Compare the upfront price in two apps before you book.

### What is the bike fare per km in Dhaka?

Pathao's rate was reported at ৳25 base plus ৳12 per km (Rest of World, 2022), later raised to ৳15 per km after the 2022 fuel price rise (Daily Star). Current official rates are not published. On Arohon, the app shows the exact fare before you book.

### Is there an EV bike ride app in Dhaka?

Yes. Arohon offers EV bike as a ride type alongside regular bikes.

### Can I pay for a bike ride with bKash?

On Arohon, rides are cash only. bKash, Nagad and card are accepted for food and medicine orders.

### Which bike app is cheapest in Dhaka?

No app is always cheapest. inDrive can be cheaper off peak if you bargain, while fixed fare apps are more predictable. Checking two apps before booking is the best way to save.
