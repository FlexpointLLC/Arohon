Many women in Dhaka would rather ride behind a woman than a man. That is not a small preference. Many simply feel more comfortable that way, especially on a motorbike. So it makes sense that people search for a women only ride app. The problem is that the answer keeps changing. Services launch with a lot of attention, then go quiet. This guide sets out what we could actually verify for 2026, and what to do when no women only option is available near you.

## Why women only ride apps matter

On a bike, the passenger sits very close to the rider for the whole trip. In a car or CNG at night, a passenger may be alone with a stranger. Women only services try to remove that worry by matching female passengers with female riders.

The hard part has always been supply. There are far fewer women riding motorbikes for income in Bangladesh than men, and every women only service has run into that limit.

## The women only services, one by one

### OBON by OBhai

OBON is the women only service from OBhai, a Bangladeshi ride app. OBhai introduced OBON as a bike service in which a female passenger rides with a female rider, with an SOS button and regular training for riders, according to Daily Star coverage at launch.

Its history is uneven. TBS reported in 2021 that women only ride sharing apps were "stepping on the brakes", and OBON was suspended in Dhaka during the Covid period because of a shortage of female riders. A Daily Star report dated November 2025 says OBhai brought OBON back to the capital as a separate women only ride hailing app.

We could not find published data on how many female riders OBON has today, or which areas it covers in 2026. Older articles mention figures between 50 and 150 riders, but those numbers are years old. Check the OBhai app or its helpline before relying on it for a regular commute.

### Lily

Lily was reported in the press as a women only ride sharing app aimed at female passengers and female bikers. We could not confirm that Lily is still taking rides in 2026. If you find it in the app store, check recent reviews and update dates before you depend on it.

### Pink SAM

Pink SAM was another women focused service covered in Bangladeshi media. Business Post reported that a women only ride sharing service came to a halt, and we could not find evidence of Pink SAM operating today. Treat it as not currently available unless you can confirm otherwise.

## What to look for in any app

Even if no women only option is near you, most of the safety you need comes from the app itself. Before you book, check that the app gives you these:

1. **Driver details before pickup.** The driver's name, photo and number plate, so you know exactly who to expect.
2. **Live trip sharing.** A link a family member or friend can open to follow your ride in real time.
3. **Upfront fare.** Knowing the price before you book means no arguing at the door, which is often where awkward situations start.
4. **A clear way to report a problem.** Support or an in app help option you can reach quickly.

## Practical safety habits for female passengers

1. **Match the plate.** Never get on a bike or into a car until the plate matches the one in the app.
2. **Share before you start.** Send your live trip to someone at home as soon as the ride begins.
3. **Sit where you are comfortable.** In a car, the back seat behind the passenger side gives you more space and an easy exit.
4. **Choose your pickup point.** A busy, well lit spot is better than a quiet lane outside your building at night.
5. **Keep your phone charged.** Trip sharing only helps if your phone is on.
6. **Trust your instinct.** If something feels wrong at pickup, cancel and book again. Your safety is worth more than a few minutes.

## Bike, CNG or car for women at night?

A bike is fast and cheap, but it puts you very close to the rider and offers little protection in rain. A CNG gives you a closed seat and a lockable door on most vehicles. A car gives the most privacy and comfort. Late at night, many women choose a car or CNG over a bike for that reason, even if it costs a little more. ## The honest picture in 2026

Women only ride sharing in Bangladesh is still small. The idea is popular, but the number of female riders has not kept up, and services have paused more than once. OBON's 2025 relaunch is the most recent sign of life we could verify. Until women only options grow, the best protection is a mix of good app safety features and simple habits: check the plate, share your trip and pay a fixed fare you agreed to before you got on.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a women only ride sharing app in Bangladesh?

Yes, OBON by OBhai is the one we could verify. A Daily Star report from November 2025 says it relaunched in Dhaka as a women only app. Its current coverage and rider numbers are not published, so check the app first.

### Is OBON ride still available in Dhaka?

It was relaunched in Dhaka in late 2025, after an earlier suspension during Covid due to a shortage of female riders. Availability can depend on your area and time of day.

### Can I get a lady driver for a bike ride in Dhaka?

Only on a women only service like OBON, and supply is limited. Most general ride apps do not let you pick the driver's gender.
