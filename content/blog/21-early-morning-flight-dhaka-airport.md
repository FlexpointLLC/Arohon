Your flight leaves at 6 AM. That means the alarm goes off in the middle of the night, the roads are dark, and the question keeps you awake: will I actually find a ride at 2 or 3 AM? Missing an early flight is expensive, and the worry often ruins the last night at home.

The fix is simple planning. This guide shows you how to work backwards from your departure time, what to prepare the night before, and how to get to Shahjalal Airport without rushing.

## Work backwards from your departure time

Start with the flight and count back. Airlines publish their own check in times, and most advise arriving around 3 hours early for international flights and around 1 to 2 hours early for domestic flights. Always check your airline's own instruction, as it overrides any general rule.

Here is an example for a 6 AM international flight:

| Step | Time |
|---|---|
| Flight departs | 6:00 AM |
| Check in counter closes (check your airline) | Usually about 1 hour before |
| Aim to reach the terminal | About 3:00 AM |
| Travel time from home | Depends on your area |
| Leave home | About 2:00 to 2:30 AM from most areas |

For a domestic 6 AM flight, you can usually arrive later, around 4:30 to 5:00 AM, but check your airline.

## How long will the road take at night?

Night traffic in Dhaka is lighter than in the day, but not always empty. Trucks move through the city at night, and there can be road works on key routes. As a rough guide, leave more time than you think:

1. Uttara and nearby areas are closest to the airport.
2. Gulshan, Banani and Mohakhali are usually a short trip at night.
3. Mirpur and Dhanmondi need more time.
4. Old Dhaka and areas south need the most.

If you are unsure, add an extra 30 minutes. Waiting at the terminal is far better than watching the clock in traffic.

## The night before checklist

Do these things before you sleep:

1. Confirm your flight time and terminal with your airline.
2. Keep your passport, ticket and visa documents in one bag.
3. Weigh your luggage so there are no surprises at check in.
4. Set two alarms.
5. Book or plan your ride, and know your pickup point.
6. Charge your phone and keep some cash ready for the ride.

## Which terminal are you using?

Shahjalal Airport has older terminals and the newer Terminal 3 building. Which terminal you use depends on your airline and when it starts operating there, so check your ticket or the airline's notice the night before. Telling your driver the right terminal saves time at the drop off area, especially when you are close to the counter closing time.

## Choosing the right vehicle at night

| Situation | Suggested vehicle |
|---|---|
| Solo traveller, small bag | Car, or bike if luggage is very light |
| Couple with suitcases | Car |
| Family with several bags | Car plus or micro |
| Large group or extended family seeing you off | Micro or Hiace |

At night with luggage, a car is usually the safer and more comfortable choice than a bike.

## Safety at night

1. Check the driver's name, photo and plate before you get in.
2. Share your trip with a family member so someone knows you are on the way.
3. Wait inside your building until the car arrives.
4. Keep your passport on you, not in the boot.

## Frequently asked questions

### When should I leave home for a 6 AM flight from Dhaka?

For an international flight, most people aim to reach the terminal about 3 hours early, so leaving around 2:00 to 2:30 AM works from most areas. Always check your airline's check in time.

### Can I find a ride to the airport at 3 AM?

Plan ahead the night before, know your pickup point, and leave extra time in case it takes a few minutes for a driver to accept.

### Which terminal is my flight using?

It depends on the airline. Check your ticket or airline notice the night before.

### How do I pay for the airport ride?

### Is a bike safe for an airport trip at night?

For a solo traveller with a very small bag it can work, but with luggage at night a car is the more comfortable and safer choice.
